# Elevating E1–E4: Solid-Angle Factor from the Effective Action

**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Status:** Controlled effective-action argument. Shows *under what conditions* \(W_\star=1/(4\pi)\) emerges from the Proca + Einstein system. Does **not** claim a unique theorem without those conditions.

---

## 1. Starting Point

From the written action:
\[
G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G\bigl(T_{\mu\nu} + W\, T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm info}\bigr)
\]
with Proca kinetic term, non-minimal \(\rho_b^\alpha\) coupling, and \(|A|^4\) saturation.

## 2. Screen Reduction

On a spatial ball with boundary \(\partial B\cong S^2\), the quasi-static boundary effective action for the radial condensate \(\varphi=A_r|_{\partial B}\) takes the form
\[
S_\partial[\varphi] = \frac{1}{2g^2}\int_{\partial B}\varphi\,\mathcal{K}\,\varphi - \int_{\partial B} J_{\rm info}\,\varphi
\]
with DtN operator \(\mathcal{K}\).

## 3. Spherical-Harmonic Normalization

Monopole mode:
\[
Y_{00} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi}},\qquad
\varphi = \bar\varphi\, Y_{00} = \frac{\bar\varphi}{\sqrt{4\pi}}.
\]
Canonical normalization of \(\bar\varphi\) produces a tree-level effective coupling
\[
W_{\rm eff} = \frac{c_\star}{4\pi}
\]
where \(c_\star\) absorbs DtN eigenvalue and wave-function renormalization.

**Canonical matching** (\(c_\star=1\)):
\[
W_\star = \frac{1}{4\pi}.
\]

E2–E4 are thereby reduced to a matching convention plus the existence of the screen (E1).
